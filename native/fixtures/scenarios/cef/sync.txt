# Two pages of one site in one sync set, 800 and 400 wide. Run by sync.sh,
# which serves the site and writes the canvas. With the camera it sets, the
# wide page is at screen x 40..640 and the narrow one at x 715..1015, both
# y 80..455, at 0.75 of a CSS pixel.
snapshot 01-loaded.png
# Enter the wide page and click Menu: the narrow page's Menu button is
# elsewhere, and its panel opens too.
click 300 300
click 300 300
move 100 110
wait 500
click 100 110
wait 1500
snapshot 02-menu.png
# Follow the link: both pages go to the second page.
click 250 110
wait 2500
snapshot 03-navigated.png
# Back from the wide page takes the narrow one back.
act page-back
wait 2500
snapshot 04-back.png
# Scroll the wide page: the narrow page, a longer document, goes to the
# same fraction of its own length, which is further in pixels.
wheel 0 -1200
wait 1500
snapshot 05-scrolled.png
key escape
