# canvas: guides.canvas
# Alignment and distribution guides: a shape dragged level with two others
# shows the lines along its edges and the measures of the even gaps, a copy
# lines up with its original, a resize confirms the edge its handle moves,
# and the lines keep their weight zoomed out. Nothing pulls: the grid does.
camera 0,0,1
expect saved same-as guides.canvas

# a press that has not travelled shows nothing
press 450 550
expect guides none
# level with left and right, midway: lines top and bottom, two gap measures
drag-to 450 250
expect guides horizontal top/left.top 200 bottom/left.bottom 300 top/right.top 200 bottom/right.bottom 300 top/tall.top 200
expect gaps horizontal 100 | 300 400 250 | 500 600 250
# a centre shows only where the grid allows it: these are 100 wide
expect guides vertical none
# the lines along its top and bottom edges and the two even gaps' measures
snapshot 01-aligned-and-even.png
# the grid rounds 7 short back onto the line
drag-to 450 257
expect guides horizontal top/left.top 200 bottom/left.bottom 300 top/right.top 200 bottom/right.bottom 300 top/tall.top 200
# a little off the row: the grid has not put it on the line, so no guides
drag-to 450 262
expect guides horizontal none
# one row down: its top on their bottom, and its middle on the tall one's,
# which is kept because their edges do not both agree
drag-to 450 350
expect guides horizontal top/left.bottom 300 top/right.bottom 300 middle/tall.middle 350
drag-to 450 250
release
expect guides none
expect entity a rect 400 200 100 100
key cmd+z
expect saved same-as start

# an Option-drag: the copy below lines up with the original's sides
press 450 550
hold alt
drag-to 450 750
expect guides vertical left/a:origin.left 400 right/a:origin.right 500
hold none
drag-to 450 550
release

# a resize: the right handle reaching the tall shape's left edge
select left
press 300 250
drag-to 900 250
expect entity left rect 200 200 700 100
expect guides vertical right/tall.left 900
expect guides horizontal none
release
key cmd+z

# zoomed out the lines are still a pixel and the caps the same height

release
