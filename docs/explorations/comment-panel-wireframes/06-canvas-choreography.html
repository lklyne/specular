06Canvas ↔ panel choreography

Moving the thread off-canvas only works if the spatial link stays tight. The storyboard: what happens on canvas when the panel acts, and vice versa. Badges and the inline creation composer stay on canvas.

1 · Badge click. Badge stays the on-canvas presence of a thread. Clicking it opens (or focuses) the panel on that thread…
→
2 · …and the anchor lights up. Element outline pulses; if scrolled away, the page scroll-reveals it (existing reveal orchestrator). Selecting a thread in the panel does the same.
→
3 · Creation stays inline. The pending composer still appears at the click point — spatial context matters most at creation. On submit it closes and the thread lives in the panel.
→
4 · Hover, both directions. Hover a badge → its row/thread highlights in the panel. Hover a thread row → badge and outline glow on canvas. Cheap, and it replaces most of what the popup's in-place-ness provided.
