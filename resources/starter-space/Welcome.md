# What you can do here

- **Pull any site onto the canvas.** Paste a URL. It stays live — scroll it, click it, log in.
- **See one page at every size.** Put the same URL side by side at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.
- **Mark things up.** Draw, drop a sticky, or leave a comment right on the page.
- **Hand it to an agent.** Your notes and comments are files on disk, so Claude can read them and make the change.

Everything lives in your space folder as plain files — `.canvas`, `.md`, `.png`. No account, nothing uploaded.
